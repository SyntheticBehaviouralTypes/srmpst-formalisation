Agda formalisation of a *Synthetic Reconstruction of Multiparty Session Types*.
Branch: `simplified-theory`.

## Organizing idea

The metatheory is **parameterized over an abstract behavioral theory** rather than
fixing global types up front:

- Safety/liveness are proved against an axiomatized LTS (`BTheory` + `WellBehaved`).
- A concrete finite-graph LTS is shown, separately, to satisfy those axioms.

So the safety theorems hold for *any* well-behaved theory, and graphs are just one
instance.

Three top-level modules compile (`runall.sh` builds every root `*.agda`):
`Definitions.agda`, `LTS.agda`, `Safety.agda`.

## 1. Abstract core — `Definitions/` (parameterized by `N` = #participants)

- **`Common.agda`** — `Part = Fin N`; a `Label` is a tag among `suc I` choices.
- **`Actions.agda`** — `Action = Comm (sender ⟶ receiver) # Choice (label < sort >)`;
  membership `_∈α_`/`_∉α_` and the **independence relation `_⋄_`** (two comms are
  independent when neither's receiver is involved in the other). `⋄` drives the
  concurrency/diamond reasoning.
- **`Expr.agda`** — tiny expression language (bool/nat/unit, `minus1`, `is-zero`),
  big-step eval `_⇓_`, typing `⊢e`, expression preservation/progress lemmas.
- **`Behav.agda`** — the heart of the abstraction:
  - `record BTheory` = carrier `Behav` + labelled transition `_-<_>->_`.
  - Derived: participation (`_∈T_`, `ended`), the **skip relation `_-[¬ P ]->*_`**
    (steps not involving `P`), coinductive **bisimilarity `_~_`** (`≲` simulation)
    with equivalence/congruence lemmas.
  - `record WellBehaved` = axiom bundle the metatheory needs: determinism
    (`step-deterministic`, sort/arity-deterministic), `step-is-prop`,
    `sender≢receiver`, **diamond** (`step-diamond`), bisimulation step-back, and
    "no new communication/branch appears out of nowhere" axioms. Derives
    `skip/advance`, `branch/before`, etc.
- **`Proc.agda`** — process calculus `Proc γ δ` (`γ` expr binders, `δ` recursion
  binders): send, `Σ`-guarded receive (branching), `if`, `rec`/`var`, `∅`. All
  substitution/weakening machinery, plus **session operational semantics**
  `_[_]⇒_` (labelled `s/comm`, silent `s/if`/`s/rec`), `τ⇒`, divergence `_⇒∞`,
  `done`/`finished`.
- **`Typing.agda`** (`module MPST wb`) — typing judgment `Γ & Δ ⊢p P ◂ Pr ∶ G`
  ("process `Pr` implements participant `P` of behavior `G`"). Notable:
  - **`skip` sub-judgment** `⊢skip[ prod/nonprod ]` with productivity modes to rule
    out vacuous cycles.
  - `t/unskip` (typing up to inactive prefixes), `t/rec` requires `MessageGuarded`,
    `t/var` closes recursion up to `~`.
  - **Head normal form** judgment `⊢head` + `head/typing` connecting the two.
  - `⊢s M ∶ G` types a whole session.
- **`Guard.agda`, `TypeChecker.agda`** (~602 lines, decidable checker),
  **`Types.agda`** (~1174 lines, concrete guarded `Global` type syntax). NOTE:
  `Types.agda` is currently self-contained and *not* re-exported by
  `Definitions.agda` — looks like the in-progress concrete typechecking front-end
  (matches HEAD "Towards typechecking and LTS decidability").

## 2. Concrete instance — `LTS/` (parameterized by `N`)

Finite-graph realization of `BTheory`:

- **`Core.agda`** — `Graph` = adjacency list over `Fin size`;
  `graphTheory G : BTheory N` uses reachable edges as transitions.
- **`Action.agda`** — decidable equality on actions/choices/comms.
- **`Algebra.agda`, `Reachability.agda`, `Bisimulation.agda`** (~861 lines),
  **`Decision.agda`, `WellBehaved.agda`** (~633 lines) — payoff is
  `wellBehaved : ... → WellBehaved (graphTheory G)`, discharging the abstract
  axioms for concrete graphs, plus decidability of bisimilarity/reachability.

## 3. Metatheory — `Safety/` + `Typing/Substitution.agda`

All parameterized by `(wb : WellBehaved B)`, so they hold for any well-behaved
theory (graphs included):

- **`Typing/Substitution.agda`** — substitution lemmas for the typing judgment.
- **`Safety/Head.agda`, `Skip.agda`** — convert general typing to head-normal typing.
- **`Safety/Preservation.agda`** — `preservation` (subject reduction): every
  `M [ α ]⇒ M′` yields `G′` with `G -< α >-> G′` (or `G` for silent steps),
  preserving `⊢s`; lifted to `preservation/τ*`.
- **`Safety/Progress.agda`** — `progress`: a well-typed session is either `done`
  or can step.
- **`Safety/Termination.agda`** — `no-infinite-τ-reductions`: no well-typed session
  diverges on silent steps (well-founded recursion on a `τ-depth` measure).

Progress + preservation + termination give the safety/liveness story.

## Mental model (one line)

> Prove type safety once against an axiomatized LTS (`BTheory`/`WellBehaved`),
> instantiate with a concrete decidable graph LTS. `Definitions` = abstract
> syntax + typing, `LTS` = concrete model discharging the axioms, `Safety` = the
> metatheorems.
